# NACA 0012, Re = 6 million: WindLab vs NASA TMR

Generated by `scripts/validate_naca0012.py` on 2026-11-03 (aarch64 Linux container, 4 MPI ranks). Raw numbers: `naca0012_tmr.json`.

Setup: 2D (one cell thick, empty front/back, no tips), sharp-trailing-edge TMR NACA 0012, chord 1 m, U = 50 m/s, nu = 8.33333e-06 m^2/s (Re = 6e+06, Mach 0.147, incompressible solver), simpleFoam k-omega SST, fully turbulent, freestream Tu = 0.1 %, nu_t/nu = 0.2. Structured C-grid from blockMesh, 184,728 cells, far field 500 chords, first cell 2e-06 c: **wall-resolved (y+ < 1), no wall functions**.

Reference values: NASA TMR SST results of CFL3D and FUN3D on the finest (897x257) grid, <https://tmbwg.github.io/turbmodels/naca0012_val_sst.html> (read 2026-11-03). At 0 deg the TMR gives CL as approximately 0. Acceptance criterion of the project: CL within 2 % and CD within 5 % at 10 deg, |CL| < 0.01 at 0 deg, 15 deg indicative only. **The check column judges the raw WindLab value against the mean of the two TMR codes** (columns *vs mean*).

TMR ran compressible at M = 0.15, WindLab is incompressible. To first order (Prandtl-Glauert) compressibility raises CL by 1.0114 at this Mach number; the column *vs mean (PG)* compares with the TMR mean divided by that factor, i.e. with its incompressible equivalent. It is indicative only and plays no part in the check.
